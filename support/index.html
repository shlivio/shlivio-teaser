SHLIVIO Support

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Need help with the SHLIVIO app? Email support@shlivio.com and we will get back to you. Please write from the email address you use to sign in, and tell us which phone you use (iPhone or Android).

Signing in

SHLIVIO uses Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. If you signed up with Apple and chose to hide your email, write to us from the address you can reach and tell us that you used Sign in with Apple.

Your SHLIVIO Day

A SHLIVIO Day is 24 hours off your phone. Press Start my SHLIVIO Day to share that you are going offline, pick what you are doing, and after 24 hours press End my SHLIVIO Day to share a recap. You can also start a SHLIVIO Day with up to five friends using an invite link.

Connected accounts

With full SHLIVIO, your notice and recap can post for you automatically to LinkedIn, X, Instagram Professional (Business or Creator) accounts and Facebook Pages. Personal Instagram and Facebook profiles cannot be connected; you can still share to them yourself from the share sheet.

You can disconnect an account at any time in the app. Disconnecting does not delete posts already published; delete those on the platform itself.

Subscriptions

SHLIVIO Lite is free. Full SHLIVIO is an auto-renewing subscription that you buy, manage and cancel through your app store, not through us:

  • iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Android: open the Google Play Store, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time. See Delete Your SHLIVIO Account for the steps and what happens to your data. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription; cancel it in your app store as well.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Privacy questions can be directed to privacy@shlivio.com.
