Delete Your SHLIVIO Account

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in SHLIVIO, the mobile app provided by Shlivio Inc., and what happens to your data when you do.

How to delete your account

In the App: open the App's menu, tap "Delete account," and confirm. Your account is deleted right away. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

By email: if you can no longer open the App, email privacy@shlivio.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account information, including your email address and display name.
  • Your activity log entries and your memberships in shared SHLIVIO Days.
  • Your connected social media accounts, which are disconnected, and the records of those connections, including account-connection security logs.
  • The authorizations our posting provider stores for your connected social media accounts, which it deletes when we disconnect them.
  • Your beta invitation and admission records and any invite codes issued to you.
  • Your push notification token and notification delivery records.
  • Your share-open records.

Residual copies may persist in routine backups for up to 7 days, until those backups are cycled out.

What is kept

  • A record of each broadcast attempt (the platform, the time, the outcome, and its cost to us), stored under a coded identifier in place of your account identifier, for cost and operations reporting.
  • Records our posting provider sent us about the status of posts, with the link to your account removed, for security, accounting, and dispute resolution.
  • Aggregated, de-identified statistics about how the App is used, which do not identify you.

None of these contain your name, email address, or account identifier.

Notification delivery records are deleted automatically 30 days after the notification is sent at the latest, even if you keep your account.

Posts already published to a social media platform stay there until you delete them on that platform. A reconnection card image uploaded for an automatic post is hosted by our posting provider for up to 60 days after upload and is then deleted. Deleting your account does not shorten this period.

If you host a shared SHLIVIO Day, it closes when you delete your account, and the history other members contributed stays available to them.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy. Questions can be directed to privacy@shlivio.com.
